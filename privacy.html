Privacy notice

Last updated: September 11, 2026

Scope and operator

This notice applies to the MellowCamp company information website (mellowcamp.cn), operated by Guangzhou Yuxiang Trading Co., Ltd.

Website visits

This website has no registration, payment, contact form or advertising account authorization features. Its page code does not set cookies or include analytics scripts or advertising trackers. Static hosting and network providers may process IP addresses, browser information and access logs to deliver and secure their services, under their own privacy notices.

Email enquiries

When you email us, we receive your email address and the information you include. We use this information to respond, follow up on your enquiry and keep necessary correspondence records. Please do not send passwords, API secrets or refresh tokens. Email is processed by the relevant email service providers.

External websites

This website links to our Amazon store. Following a link takes you to Amazon, whose own terms and privacy notices govern its data collection, cookies, purchases and account services.

Internal advertising tool

We are developing an advertising tool for internal company use and applying for Ads API access. This website does not connect advertising accounts or retrieve advertising reports. Data-handling arrangements for the future integration will be described separately based on its actual functionality; this website notice is not a complete data-processing policy for that tool.

Retention, disclosure and requests

We retain correspondence as needed to handle enquiries and maintain necessary records. We do not sell personal information contained in enquiries. Information may be processed by hosting, network or email service providers, or disclosed when required by law. Contact us to request access to, correction of or deletion of information you have provided. We will address requests based on the information we hold and applicable requirements, verifying identity when needed.

Updates and contact

We will update this notice when website features or handling practices change. For privacy enquiries, email service.mellowcamp@gmail.com.
