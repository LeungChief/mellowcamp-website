Website privacy notice.

How information is handled when you browse this website or contact our team.

Who we are

Guangzhou Yuxiang Trading Co., Ltd.（广州市昱翔商贸有限公司） operates the MellowCamp brand website. Contact: service.mellowcamp@gmail.com.

Browsing the website

This is an informational website. We do not provide user accounts, checkout or a contact form. The site does not use advertising cookies or third-party analytics scripts. Product filters, menus and image viewers operate in your browser without sending their selections to us.

Hosting and technical information

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub and network providers may process technical request information, such as an IP address and request logs, to deliver and protect their services. Website photographs and product images are served from this website; browsing does not require loading them from the original photography platforms.

GitHub Privacy Statement

When you email us

If you choose to contact us, we receive your email address and the contents you send. We use them to respond to the enquiry and follow up as needed. Please send only relevant information and avoid including passwords or payment card details.

Email correspondence is retained as needed to address enquiries and meet applicable recordkeeping obligations. You can contact us to ask about your correspondence or request correction or deletion, subject to obligations that may require retention.

Links to other websites

Links to Amazon, photography sources or other external sites lead to services with their own privacy practices and terms. Their policies apply when you visit them.

Updates

We may update this notice when website features or handling practices change. Last updated: September 12, 2026.
