Internal technology

Internal use · Integration in development

Supporting our own advertising operations

We are developing an internal operations tool and applying for Amazon Ads API access for our company’s own advertising accounts. The tool is not currently offered as an advertising management service or software subscription to other businesses.

Intended uses

  1. Consolidate campaign and performance reports from our authorized advertising accounts.
  2. Analyze advertising spend, attributed sales, ACOS and ROAS, and monitor budget usage.
  3. Generate optimization recommendations and manage campaigns, bids and budgets after the relevant access and internal authorization are in place.

The Ads API integration is in the application and development stage. These are intended uses, not a claim of Amazon approval, certification or partner status. This website does not provide advertising account sign-in or authorization.
