01 / Choose your comfort.

Start with how you like to unwind. A higher back and removable pillow may suit long pauses at a campsite. A smaller packed size and lower weight may matter more when you carry your chair farther.

For a table, think about surface space and seating height. Check both the open and packed dimensions against your car, RV storage or carrying bag.

Compare our chairs↗

02 / Keep the list simple.

  • The right chair or table, with all parts and its carrying bag.
  • Water, snacks and any weather-appropriate personal items.
  • A small cloth and a bag for carrying your litter back out.
  • The product instructions and a quick check of the day’s conditions.

03 / A moment before you sit.

Follow the instructions supplied with your specific model. Fully seat all frame connections and attach the fabric or tabletop as directed. Keep fingers clear of folding joints.

Use firm, level ground. Check that all feet are stable before loading the furniture. Never stand on a chair or use a side table as a seat; keep within your model’s stated limits.

Good questions.

How do I choose between the two chairs?
Compare the weight, packed dimensions and backrest design. The high-back chair includes a removable pillow and armrests; the lightweight chair focuses on compact packing. See the chair comparison.
Where can I find the exact specifications?
Each product page lists its variant and key dimensions or materials, with a link to the corresponding Amazon listing. Use the specific model’s supplied instructions for setup and limitations.
Can I use the furniture on sand?
Soft or uneven surfaces can reduce stability. Choose a firm, level area and check every foot before use. We do not claim that these products are suitable for every sand or ground condition.
Who can help with an order or product issue?
For an Amazon order, you can use the order page to contact the seller or check available order support. For product questions, email our team with the product name and a short description. Contact MellowCamp.
